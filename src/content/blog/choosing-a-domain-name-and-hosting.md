Your domain name and hosting are easy to overlook, but problems with either can take your website and email offline. Here is what to consider.

## Choosing a domain name

A good domain name is:

- **Short and memorable**, so people can type it from a business card
- **Easy to spell**, avoiding unusual spellings and too many hyphens
- **Relevant to your business name**, so customers know they are in the right place

For a business focused on South African customers, a `.co.za` domain is a natural choice. You may also want to register the `.com` version to protect your name.

## Make sure you own it

Your domain should be registered in your business's name, with contact details you control. If a former supplier or employee registered it in their own name, you may struggle to renew or move it. Ask your provider to confirm the registrant details.

## What to look for in hosting

- **Reliability**: your site should be available consistently
- **Performance**: hosting resources that suit your site's size and traffic
- **Security**: SSL certificates, server updates and backups
- **Support**: someone to help when something goes wrong
- **Location**: hosting or a content delivery network close to your visitors can help pages load faster

## Remember the recurring costs

Domains and hosting are ongoing services, normally renewed monthly or annually. Put renewal dates in your calendar or use a provider that manages renewals for you. An expired domain can stop your website and email working, and recovering it can be difficult.
