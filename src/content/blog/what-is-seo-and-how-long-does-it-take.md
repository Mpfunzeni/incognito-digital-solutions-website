Search engine optimisation (SEO) is the work of making your website easier for search engines to understand and more useful for the people who find it. Done well, it helps potential customers discover your business when they are actively looking for what you offer.

## The three parts of SEO

### Technical SEO

Search engines need to be able to crawl and index your pages. Technical SEO covers things like:

- Pages that load quickly and work well on mobile
- A clear site structure and an XML sitemap
- Correct use of page titles, headings and canonical URLs
- Fixing broken links and pages that return errors
- HTTPS, so the site loads securely

### On-page and content SEO

Each important page should clearly answer a specific search intent. A page about website design should explain the service, who it is for, what is included and how to get started. Thin pages that repeat the same text with a different town name rarely help.

### Local and off-site signals

For businesses serving a particular area, consistent business details across the web and a well-maintained Google Business Profile help search engines connect your business with local searches. Links from relevant, reputable websites also signal trust.

## How long does it take?

Honest answer: it depends. Factors include how competitive your industry is, the current state of your website and how much useful content you have.

- **Technical fixes** can be picked up as soon as search engines recrawl the affected pages.
- **New content** needs time to be indexed and evaluated.
- **Authority and trust** build gradually over months.

Be wary of anyone who guarantees first-page rankings or overnight results. Search engines decide rankings, and their algorithms change regularly.

## How to measure progress

Google Search Console is free and shows how often your pages appear in search results, how many people click through and whether there are indexing problems. Pair it with analytics to see which visitors go on to make an enquiry.
