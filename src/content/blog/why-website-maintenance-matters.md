Launching your website is a milestone, not the finish line. Like a vehicle, a website needs regular servicing to stay safe and reliable.

## What maintenance involves

- **Software updates**: platforms, plugins and code libraries receive security patches and improvements
- **Backups**: regular copies of your site and data, stored safely, so you can recover from mistakes or attacks
- **Security monitoring**: checking for vulnerabilities, suspicious activity and expiring SSL certificates
- **Uptime monitoring**: alerts when the site becomes unavailable
- **Content updates**: keeping services, prices, team details and contact information accurate
- **Performance checks**: making sure pages still load quickly as content grows

## Why it matters

Outdated software is one of the most common ways websites are compromised. A hacked or broken website can damage customer trust, interrupt enquiries and harm your search visibility.

Stale content causes quieter problems: old prices, discontinued services or a phone number that no longer works all cost you enquiries.

## Do it yourself or use a plan?

Doing it yourself can work if someone in your team has the time and technical confidence to test updates and restore backups. A maintenance plan makes sense if you would rather have a specialist handle it, with agreed response times and a single point of contact.
